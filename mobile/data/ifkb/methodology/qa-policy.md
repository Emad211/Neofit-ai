# IFKB quality and scientific review policy

## Canonical basis

Nutrient comparison and release values should be expressed per 100 g edible portion. Household measures and servings are additional views and require a defensible gram weight.

## Food identity gate

Before assigning nutrients, curators must define the food name, aliases, region, preparation state, edible portion, recipe/product variant, and match quality. Raw and cooked forms are separate foods. A generic dish name cannot silently represent all household or restaurant recipes.

## Source hierarchy

1. Iranian laboratory analyses with documented sampling and method.
2. Licensed Iranian national composition tables and exact authoritative matches.
3. Exact matches from authoritative international databases.
4. Reproducible recipe calculations from verified ingredients.
5. Manufacturer labels for the exact product.
6. Estimates and weak secondary sources, which remain grade E.

## Recipe calculation

A composite dish requires a versioned recipe, ingredient weights, edible-portion factors, final cooked yield, and nutrient-retention factors where relevant. The calculation must be reproducible, and the source record for every ingredient must remain traceable.

## Energy checks

Energy is compared against macro-derived energy as a QA signal, not overwritten automatically. Differences may reflect fibre, alcohol, organic acids, carbohydrate definitions, rounding, or source methods. Large discrepancies open a review issue.

## Missing data

Missing values remain blank with a reason code. They are never converted to zero. Trace and below-detection values require explicit qualifiers and analytical context.

## Licensing gate

Restricted or unclear-licence source data may be indexed as source metadata, but values must not enter distributable releases until written reuse permission is documented.

## Verification gate

A food can be `verified` only when:

- identity and preparation state are complete;
- basis and portion weight are known;
- nutrient definitions and units are harmonized;
- source and licence are documented;
- derivation is reproducible;
- automated QA passes;
- a human reviewer signs off.
