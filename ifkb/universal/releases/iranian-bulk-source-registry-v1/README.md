# Iranian bulk source registry v1

This registry supports parallel research across all 261 Iranian canonical foods. It separates four different jobs that must not be conflated:

1. discovering recipe structure and serving descriptions;
2. obtaining analytical nutrient records;
3. establishing independent recipe/source groups;
4. approving a source record for a Promotion Bundle.

A collection assignment means only that the collection is a reasonable place to search for the food. It does not mean that the food is present, that a matching row is scientifically adequate, or that reuse is permitted.

## Locked safeguards

- `automatic_approval_allowed` is false for every collection.
- Public recipe pages may contribute URLs and independently extracted structured facts; recipe prose is not redistributed.
- National or regional food-composition tables require exact identity, preparation/form and row-level provenance review.
- Restricted or permission-gated datasets remain blocked until access and reuse permission are documented.
- USDA and global analytical sources may support ingredient nutrients but do not establish Iranian recipe identity or independent recipe consensus.
- Synthetic or model-generated recipes and nutrition values are excluded.

The generated research plan joins this registry to the 261-record multilingual discovery queue and produces candidate collection assignments for each of the 12 execution batches.
